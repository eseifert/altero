# Publishing and sharing

Use **My Publications** for work you want listed on your profile. Use a
**shared-collection link** for a reading list or other collection that anyone
holding the link can open.

## My Publications

Publish one work at a time from its details or by dragging it onto
**My Publications** in your personal library.

The publishing dialog asks you to:

1. Choose whether to include files and notes.
2. Confirm that the work is yours and that you may distribute any included files.
3. Choose rights for shared files, or keep the existing **Rights** field.

Linked files cannot be published because their bytes are not on the server.
Group libraries do not have My Publications.

Change a license later using the item's **Rights** field. In My Publications,
you can show or hide individual child notes and attachments.

To stop listing a work, use the remove button in its details or press Delete
in My Publications. The work and files remain in your library. Removing a
listing does not withdraw a license you have already granted.

## Profile pages

Your profile is at `/app/u/<username>`. It lists published work, with abstracts,
rights, citations and any notes or files you chose to publish.

### Who can see it

Choose the audience under **Settings → Profile**:

| Audience | Who can open the published work |
| --- | --- |
| Anyone | Anyone, without an account; the default |
| People with an account here | People signed into this altero instance |
| Nobody | The profile is hidden; your library and publication choices remain |

These settings also govern public API access to the publications. Your own
Zotero client can still synchronize them.

## Sharing one collection

Open the collection's settings and choose **Share a link…**. Creating a link
requires write access to the library.

Choose:

- the collection alone or its subcollections too;
- whether readers can open attachment files;
- an expiry date, or no expiry.

Create the link and copy it immediately. It is shown only once. If copying
fails, select the displayed URL and copy it manually. Replace a lost link
with a new one.

Anyone holding a working link can read the shared collection without an
account. The view follows changes to that collection, but does not provide
sync access or reveal the rest of the library or its Trash.

Review existing links in the same dialog and revoke those you no longer need.
Expired, revoked or deleted links stop working. Deleting the collection also
removes its links.
